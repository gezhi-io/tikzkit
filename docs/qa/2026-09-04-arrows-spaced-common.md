# arrows.spaced common legacy tips

## Scope

This slice implements `spaced to`, `spaced latex`, `spaced latex'`, `spaced stealth`, and `spaced stealth'`, including all five `reversed` aliases. It covers straight, orthogonal, diagonal, curved, start, end, and bidirectional use. Other `arrows.spaced` aliases are outside this slice.

## Local source review

- `/usr/local/texlive/2025/texmf-dist/tex/generic/pgf/libraries/pgflibraryarrows.spaced.code.tex`: all ten aliases use the starred compatibility composition `visible[sep=0pt].space`.
- `/usr/local/texlive/2025/texmf-dist/tex/generic/pgf/basiclayer/pgfcorearrows.code.tex`: `space` paints nothing and extends `0.88pt + 0.3*linewidth`; core `to`, `to reversed`, `latex`, and `stealth` define the visible paths and backend/tip-end values.
- `/usr/local/texlive/2025/texmf-dist/tex/generic/pgf/libraries/pgflibraryarrows.code.tex`: `latex'` is fill-only; `stealth'` is fill-stroke with a round join; both reversed forms use PGF's generic x reflection and swapped extents.
- `/usr/local/texlive/2025/texmf-dist/doc/generic/pgf/pgfmanual-en-library-arrows.tex`: confirms the public lowercase legacy names and the intended extra endpoint space.

At `line width=2pt`, the invisible component is `1.48pt`. The independent `to reversed` path must not be generated by reflecting `to`; its cubic controls and extents differ. The other reversed declarations reflect the visible path in x and exchange backend with tip end.

## Third-party SVG reference

`tikztosvg` was found at `/Library/TeX/texbin/tikztosvg`. The generated SVG separates every shaft and tip into sibling `<path>` elements. At a `1.99255pt` active path width, `to` uses a `1.59404pt` tip stroke with round caps and joins. Filled tips use the nonzero fill rule; `stealth'` uses fill plus the full active stroke. Curved terminals carry a final rotation matrix rather than deforming their local path.

## Visual result

Before this slice, spaced names collapsed to ordinary `to` or `stealth`, the latex spellings selected the unrelated arrows.meta `Latex`, prime variants lost their shape, and reversed variants lost their direction. The requested endpoint gap was therefore absent.

After the repair:

- The approval flow has the same visible node-to-tip gap as MacTeX on straight and orthogonal routes. The downward `to reversed` and curved `stealth' reversed` retain their distinct silhouettes and terminal tangents.
- The commutative diagram preserves prime and reversed geometry at both path ends. Bidirectional maps shorten the shaft independently at start and end.
- The vector diagram rotates each local tip without skew. Filled, open, and fill-stroke terminals retain their source paint behavior on diagonal and curved paths.

The four-panel sheets show no missing arrows, wrong directions, line-width errors, or tip/node collisions. Remaining visible differences are text rasterization and approximately 1-3 pixel node/bounding-box variation; the arrow geometry itself follows both local references.

## Commands and parameters

Implemented and exercised: `\documentclass`, `\usepackage{tikz}`, `\usetikzlibrary{arrows,arrows.spaced,positioning}`, `\begin{tikzpicture}`, `\node`, `\draw`, `\fill`, named nodes, `right=of`, `below=of`, styles, colors, `line width`, `--`, `|-`, `to`, `bend left`, `bend right`, inline path nodes, `above`, `below`, `left`, `right`, `sloped`, start/end arrow pairs, and all ten names in this scope.

Not implemented by this slice: `spaced implies`, triangle/open-triangle families, hooks, circle/diamond/square families, brackets, left/right half arrows, and `spaced serif cm`.
